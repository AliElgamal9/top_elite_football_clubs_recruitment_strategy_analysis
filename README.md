# About The Story:
  Football clubs are the core of the sport, connecting skilled players, passionate fans, and cutting-edge strategies worldwide. Beyond being teams, they’re global communities that captivate millions, battle in top leagues, and shape football through their distinct squad-building methods. This data storytelling project dives into the 2019 recruitment strategies of five elite clubs—Real Madrid, Barcelona, Juventus, Arsenal, and Liverpool—revealing how data on age, potential, wages, and contract control reflects their unique identities. Explore the numbers behind their success!.
